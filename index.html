Jake Powers


Jake Powers is a multimedia artist currently pursuing a BA in Media Arts and Sciences with a minor in English from Arizona State University.

Their work explores relations between human experiences and emerging technological realities. Jake aims to continue these pursuits by conducting deeper research into immersive systems that utilize sonic and visual modalities. Their skills with varied software suites and curiosity for computer vision techniques are the means by which they hope to expand this exploration. They have an interest in designing interactive systems, especially in conjunction with their sculptural and fabrication capabilities, to investigate concepts in new media.
